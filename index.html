What we'll do together

♥

Every member, heard

From every college and Society — private or elite, not a favoured few. Heard, acted on, and recognised on merit.

♟

Student branches & chapters backed

Counsellors heard and approved funds released without the chasing — with real visits to serve and listen.

⚑

Simpler conferences

A single-window system for approvals, so even student branches can host quality conferences without the red tape.

⚖

Transparent funds, smooth GIEEE move

Clear approvals and reimbursements, prompt records, and guidance for members at every step of the GIEEE transition.

✉

Responsive, open leadership

Every email and call answered, prompt minutes and a shared calendar — decisions taken with the whole committee.

🔒

Your data, respected

Member information used strictly and lawfully — in line with IEEE's privacy policy, GDPR and India's DPDP Act. Communication preferences honoured: if you've opted out, you won't be spammed.

🌍

Every institution, every language

A Section for every college and region — whatever your language or background, you belong here equally.

One Section — Everywhere

The Section and its activities should go to where the member is — not the other way around. Across every district, not just the city.

🎓

Student Congress, across every region

Our flagship Student / Society Congress rotated beyond Hyderabad — so branches everywhere can host and take part, instead of feeling cut off.

🏆

Flagship conferences for our sub-sections

Support to establish flagship conferences in the Guntur and Ananthapuramu sub-sections — growing regions hosting their own.

📍

An Executive Committee that travels

Section ExCom meetings held across about 12 locations on our geographical map — not only Hyderabad — so every member's voice is heard where they are.

🤝

A dedicated student-branch mentor

A dedicated mentor to help student branches apply for IEEE awards and funding — real guidance, not guesswork.

🌐

Every district represented

Real representation from Warangal, Tirupati, Sri City, Kurnool, Nandyal, Kadapa, Anantapur, Guntur and across our districts and student branches.

One Section, every region

From Hyderabad to Warangal, Guntur, Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Kadapa, Tirupati and Sri City — across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
